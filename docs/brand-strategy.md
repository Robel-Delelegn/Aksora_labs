# Aksora Labs Brand, IA, and Conversion Strategy

## Information Architecture

Primary pages:

1. Home
2. Services
3. Process
4. Selected Work
5. Industries
6. About
7. Contact

Home page sequence:

1. Hero with immediate clarity on what Aksora Labs does
2. Above-the-fold trust signals and placeholder client identity strip
3. Services summary
4. Why Aksora Labs
5. Process
6. Selected work
7. Industries
8. Trust and testimonials
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

This order is intentional. It answers the main buyer questions in a high-trust sequence:

- What do they do?
- Are they credible?
- Why are they better than a generic shop?
- How do they work?
- Can I see examples?
- Do they understand my context?
- Can I trust them enough to talk?

## Brand Direction

Desired brand signal:

- Premium
- Calm
- Precise
- Internationally credible
- Technically elite without sounding theatrical

Visual choices:

- Institutional editorial light theme inspired by the current Harvard homepage structure and restraint
- Cormorant Garamond for the headline system and Source Sans 3 for the main reading experience
- Cream paper background, white content panels, dark text, and a restrained crimson accent
- Strong horizontal rules, simpler rectangles, and less rounded UI to reduce the startup-template feel
- Large type, clean hierarchy, and sober spacing to signal authority without visual noise

The site avoids the usual agency clichés:

- No empty “innovation” language
- No manipulative urgency
- No fake logos, fake awards, or fake social proof presented as real
- No generic template section ordering

## Ethical Persuasion Architecture

Used intentionally:

- Clarity: the hero states the offer directly
- Competence signaling: typography, spacing, UI precision, and structured case studies
- Uncertainty reduction: process page, FAQ, contact guidance, and explicit delivery principles
- Authority framing: strong editorial design and specific operational language
- Status perception: premium visual rhythm, strong contrast, disciplined copy
- Risk reversal: senior-led delivery, visible milestones, maintainable architecture, support language
- Social proof structure: sample case studies, testimonial formatting, and client-logo layout ready for real references

Avoided intentionally:

- False urgency
- Fake scarcity
- Deceptive claims
- Inflated hype copy
- Artificially overcomplicated motion

## Copy Strategy

The copy is written to answer business questions rather than fill space.

Core principles:

- State what Aksora Labs does early
- Show business understanding, not just technical vocabulary
- Use concrete language around scope, reporting, architecture, and support
- Make the buyer feel the company is expensive for a reason, not expensive by posture alone
- Replace fluff with operational credibility

The CTA language is high-value but calm:

- “Book a Call”
- “View Our Work”
- “Request a discovery call”

## UX and Layout Notes

Key UX decisions:

- Announcement bar and simpler institutional header to establish tone quickly
- Sticky nav for short path to contact
- Reusable editorial page hero pattern for consistency
- Bordered information panels and FAQ disclosures for scanability
- Dedicated contact page with pre-qualification guidance
- Dynamic case study pages to make the portfolio feel real and extensible
- Generated metadata assets for stronger sharing quality

Motion approach:

- Light fade-rise on initial load
- Motion kept deliberately minimal
- Reduced-motion support included
